# Privacy Policy for YouTube Sanitiser

YouTube Sanitiser does not collect or transmit any personal data.

The only data stored is your filter preferences (which content types to hide, your minimum view count setting, and whether to exempt subscribed channels). It is saved with Chrome's built-in sync storage: if you are signed in to Chrome with sync turned on, Chrome syncs it to your other devices through your Google account; otherwise it stays in your browser. The extension itself never sends it anywhere.

When "Exclude subscribed channels" is turned on, the extension reads your subscribed channel list from the YouTube sidebar so it can exempt those channels. This list is held in memory only while the page is open and is never stored or transmitted.
